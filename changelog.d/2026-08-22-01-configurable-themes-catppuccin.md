- Bugfix: `tools/log-simulator.html` produced badly corrupted `.log` files — lines mashed together with no newline between them, and runs of stray leading whitespace before some lines (this session, 2026-08-19, person-reported, three attached files: `app-1.log` looked fine at first glance but was corrupted from partway through; `app-2.log`/`app-3.log`, created via "Start next file" rotation, were corrupted almost throughout).

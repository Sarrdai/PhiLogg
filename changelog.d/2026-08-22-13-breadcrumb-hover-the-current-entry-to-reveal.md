Hovering the active/"current" chip in `#breadcrumb` opens a flyout (`#childNavFlyoutHost`) listing its children — click one to jump straight to it, no need to open the tree sidebar. Two new Settings → Behavior toggles: `#settingsHoverChildNav` (master on/off, default ON) and `#settingsHoverChildNavMultistep` (default OFF — off shows only the immediate children; on lets hovering a child cascade a further flyout for *its* children, arbitrarily deep, "wenn On kann man auch über die Childs hovern um dann deren Childs zu sehen und so weiter").

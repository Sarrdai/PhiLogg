- **The live tailing-style view update during a file's own load was removed outright** (this session, 2026-08-28, same-day follow-up to the two entries directly below — *"Baue die Visualisierung einfach aus. Der Ladebalken genügt mir beim Laden."*). After two rounds of performance tuning (throttling, then skipping the view's most expensive per-tick scans) still left a large/fast load measurably slower with the live view on than off, and real decoupling (parsing off the main thread via a Web Worker) turned out not to be realistic for this codebase's single-file/no-build-tooling shape, the person's own preference was simpler than further tuning: drop the live view during loading entirely and keep just the progress bar. `scheduleLoadRender` is now just two direct DOM writes per tick — the loading file's own tree-row progress fill/count (`updateLoadRowLiveData`, including any filter already created under it) and the level bar's counts (`updateLevelBarCounts`) — and never a `render()` of any kind, active view or a file loading in the background alike. The Filtered/Full/minimap view for a loading file only reflects it again once the load's natural completion runs a real, full render (`flushLoadRender`), or an unrelated action (a click, a filter edit, switching away and back) calls `render()` anyway. The throttle (`LOAD_RENDER_MIN_INTERVAL_MS`), the rAF-coalescing, the per-tick main-view render function (`renderLoadTickMainView`), and the flag that made `renderTable()` skip its two expensive scans during a load tick were all removed as dead code rather than left unused. See "File loading" in `docs/persistence-and-sync.md` (rewritten to lead with current behavior, with the whole same-session arc summarized underneath instead of stacked as further "Fix N" entries). `tests/philogg.regression.test.js`: Groups 50/51 rewritten in place to assert the new invariant (no `render()` at all during any load tick, regardless of active/background); Group 52 unaffected (never depended on the removed live-view path); Groups 127/128 (the throttle and the scan-skipping) deleted outright, since the mechanisms they tested no longer exist. 2459 passed, 0 failed.

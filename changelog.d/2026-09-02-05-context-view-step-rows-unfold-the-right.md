- **Context view: step rows unfold the right way round, and the view scrolls sideways** (this session, 2026-09-02, person-reported follow-up to the entry below, with a screenshot). Four things:
  1. **The step rows' direction was inverted.** The previous fix held the *far* side of the insertion, so a block unfolded away from the button that had just been clicked: the step at the bottom of a block pushed everything above it up, the one at its top pushed the block down. The right rule is the other one — a step row **belongs to the revealed run it sits against**, and that run stays put while the block unfolds away from it. So `side: "top"` (the step at the **bottom** of a block, growing that run downwards) now pins the row *before* the filler group, and `side: "bottom"` (the step at its **top**) pins the row *after* it. "… N lines" and the run-line collapse are unchanged.
  2. **The connecting line now meets its arrowheads.** Two separate errors: the line's inset on a capped end was 13px while the chevron's ink only reaches ~8.25px down its own box, leaving a visible gap; and the caret box was offset by −5px when it needed −7px, because an absolutely positioned child is placed against its parent's **padding** box — which here starts *inside* the 2px `border-left` that draws the line, so −5px put the arrows 2px to its right. Measured in a real browser afterwards: the caret's centre and the line's axis are both at x = 281.
  3. **The Context view scrolls horizontally.** `#highlightBody` was `overflow-x:hidden` with an ellipsis; it now mirrors the Filter view's whole mechanism — `.col-msg{min-width:max-content}` extended to `#highlightRows`, `syncHighlightRowsWidth()` giving the rows an explicit width from the widest message in the list (`cachedMaxHighlightMsgPixelWidth`, recomputed in `rebuildHighlightOffsets`), and `syncHighlightHeaderWidth()` plus a `scroll` listener keeping `#highlightHeader` the same width and at the same `scrollLeft`. Unlike `TABLE_ROWS_H_PADDING`, the padding is read off the element rather than hardcoded: this one varies with `ctx-active` (the marker lane) as well as with `--color-mark-w`.
  4. **The filler labels are centred on the visible area**, not on the row — which is now the point, since a row can be far wider than the panel. The label sits in its own `.ctx-filler-inner`: `position:sticky; left:0` against the scrolling body, a negative margin undoing `#highlightRows`' own `--rows-pad-left` (extracted into a custom property for exactly this) so "pinned at 0" means the panel's left edge, and a width taken from `highlightBody.clientWidth` per render. Verified in a real browser at two scroll offsets: the label's centre and the viewport's centre are both 739 at `scrollLeft` 0 *and* at 400.

Unlike a step row, this one has no revealed run of its own to belong to — it *is* the stretch between two blocks, with a run on both sides in the normal shape, and it reveals all of it — so there is no side that is obviously "its own". The alternative considered was leaving it as it was (always hold the row above, deterministic but backwards whenever you are reading *below* the stretch); this rule is the only one that gets both directions right. `contextSelectionAtOrBelow(filler.to)` decides: a selection at or below the stretch's end holds the row after the filler group (the block being read stays and the lines appear above it), anything else holds the row before it. A selection that is absent, belongs to another file, or sits *inside* the stretch — where it has no row to be held still yet — falls back to the row above, i.e. exactly the previous behaviour.

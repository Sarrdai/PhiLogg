- **Fixed: log rows (Context/Filtered) could render narrower than the view when every message was short, making the `.selected` row outline look cut off partway across the screen** (this session, 2026-09-05, person-reported with a screenshot). `#tableRows`/`#highlightRows` already clear their own inline `width` whenever the widest message needs less room than the view — falling back to their `left:0;right:0` stretch-fill — but that decision is only ever recomputed when the entry list itself changes, not on every resize/layout pass, so a stale narrower width computed once could stick around with nothing to correct it. Added `min-width:100%` (of the positioned `#tableSpacer`/`#highlightSpacer` ancestor) to both rules as a pure-CSS floor requiring no JS timing to get right: it always wins over a smaller explicit `width` (min-width clamps upward per the CSS box model), while a genuinely wider explicit width (the real "content needs a horizontal scrollbar" case) still wins over this floor since it's larger. `tests/philogg.regression.test.js`: extended Group 73's existing "short messages leave #tableRows filling the view" assertion with a `getComputedStyle(...).minWidth === "100%"` check on both elements. **3556 passed, 0 failed** (3554 + 2).

- **Fixed: "Show M newest files" caused an unbounded merge+render loop every poll, felt laggy, and could yank a scrolled-away selection back into view** (this session, 2026-09-09, person-reported: "das Scrolling fühlt sich laggy an, wenn ein Folder Watch aktiv ist" plus a selected row snapping back while scrolling, both traced to this one setting). `folderScanTick`'s "did anything change" check compared each poll's scan against `folder.files` — but `applyFolderAutoRules`'s "Show newest M" rule (rule 1) actively drops closed listing entries beyond the newest M straight out of `folder.files`, even though nothing disappeared from disk. The very next poll's scan still found those files; since they no longer matched anything in the (now-shrunk) `folder.files`, they looked brand new, triggering `mergeScannedFiles` (which puts them back) and then `applyFolderAutoRules` again (which immediately re-hides them) — forever, every `FOLDER_SCAN_MS` (3s), for any folder with more matching files than its configured "Show newest M". Each cycle's unwanted `render()` could also snap a deliberately-scrolled-away selection straight back into view (`captureViewAnchor`/`restoreViewAnchor` prioritizing the still-selected row on every render — see `docs/ui-and-views.md` "Context view") — the exact symptom reported, even though that anchoring mechanism itself was working as designed and is unrelated to filter switches/tailing (which still need it and are unaffected). Root cause traced to `7fa9034` ("Add per-folder Folder watch settings dialog", 2026-09-03), which introduced "Show newest M" as the first auto-rule that removes entries from `folder.files` rather than just opening/closing them.
